# MAT_26_GR34

### Group number and Focus area:
Group number 34 and LCA

### Identified issue:
The issue comes from the open area in the middle of building 308. Here they choose to renovate the building and building it taller. This means that the garden area in the middle won't get any sunlight and it will therefore destroy the biodiversity. By also doing this there won't be enough light coming into the buildings ground floor. 
This will mean that they need to use more energy on the lighting in these parts of the building because of the lack of natural light.
From our own experience is the area never really used for it's correct use, which is to chill after a lecture.

### Our solution:
A solution could be to not do a renovation at all and just leave the building as it is. \
Another could be to fully renovate the garden by making it seem more attractable to be there. By doing this students have a higher chance to go out there and chill there instead of the other garden right outside of the building. \
To solve the problem with not getting enough natural lighting a solution could be making the auditorium walls taller than the rest of the building and implement ceiling windows or just windows on each side. By doing this the sun would be allowed in and the building wouldn't use as much energy on lighting.

### Report group, page number and section
All of this was found in the report made by group 9 in the report "_Part A Client Report Group 09_". The issue was discovered on page 6 in the figure text section, where they propose their first building design.
